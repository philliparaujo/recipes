Method

  1. Pat the steak very dry and rub with the salt, black pepper and garlic powder. Let it sit at room temperature while you do the potatoes.
  2. Cover the cubed potatoes with cold water in the saucepan, add the salt, bring to a boil, then simmer until just fork-tender at the edges.
  3. Drain well and steam-dry in a colander for a couple of minutes. Toss gently with the paprika, onion powder, garlic powder and salt.
  4. Heat the oil in the frypan over medium-high. Add the potatoes in a single layer and turn only every 3 to 4 minutes, until deeply golden and crisp, about 11 minutes. Move to a plate and cover loosely.
  5. Wipe out the pan, add the oil for the steak, and heat until shimmering but not smoking, since the nonstick coating does not like extreme heat. Cook the steak 3 to 4 minutes per side for medium-rare (130 to 135°F), and sear the fat edge for 30 seconds.
  6. Rest the steak 5 minutes and drizzle with the finishing oil. Slice against the grain and serve over the potatoes.

Notes

  • Swap in Italian seasoning on the potatoes for a herby version, or toss them with a little Frank's RedHot after crisping.
  • If your steak is thinner than 1", cut the sear time to 2 to 3 minutes per side.
  • A 1 lb strip serves two when sliced.
