Method

  1. Fill the Dutch oven with about 2.5 quarts of water, add the salt, cover, and bring to a boil.
  2. Toss the cubed chicken with the salt, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, Italian seasoning and black pepper.
  3. Cook the chicken in the oil in the frypan over medium-high in a single layer, stirring occasionally, until golden outside and 165°F inside. Move it to a plate and wipe out any burnt bits from the pan.
  4. Cook the penne 1 minute less than the package says, adding the broccoli for the last 2 to 3 minutes. Scoop out 1/2 cup of starchy pasta water, then lift the penne and broccoli out with the slotted spoon, letting each scoop drip, into a large bowl.
  5. Pour the Alfredo into the frypan over low heat with about half the pasta water and stir until warm, about 2 minutes. Do not let it boil, since Alfredo can turn grainy or separate when overheated.
  6. Add the penne, broccoli florets and chicken to the sauce (or pour the sauce over them in the bowl), stir in the parmesan, and toss until coated. Loosen with more pasta water if thick, because the sauce thickens a lot in the fridge.
  7. Divide into 4 equal portions. Eat one now; cool the rest uncovered for 20 to 30 minutes, then cover and refrigerate.

Notes

  • Keeps 3 to 4 days in the fridge. To reheat, add 1 to 3 tbsp milk or water, cover loosely, and microwave at 70% power for 90 seconds, stir, then another 60 to 90 seconds until steaming hot (165°F). Stir well before and halfway through so the sauce re-blends instead of separating.
  • With potatoes instead of broccoli: eat the lunches earlier in the week, since russets can turn slightly mealy after the second day, and don't freeze it, because cooked potatoes turn grainy after thawing.
