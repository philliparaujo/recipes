Method

  1. Fill the Dutch oven with about 2.5 quarts of water (lighter to drain), add the salt, cover, and bring to a boil.
  2. Warm the oil in the frypan over medium-high. Add the beef with the salt, onion powder, garlic powder, paprika, black pepper and red pepper flakes. Break it up and cook until browned with no pink. At 91/9 there is little fat, so only drain if it looks greasy.
  3. Stir the Italian seasoning into the beef for 30 seconds, then pour in the marinara. Simmer uncovered over medium-low for 12 to 15 minutes, stirring often, until it coats the back of a spoon.
  4. Meanwhile, cook the penne 1 minute less than the package says, adding the broccoli for the last 2 to 3 minutes. Scoop both out with the slotted spoon, letting each scoop drip over the pot, then drain thoroughly.
  5. Fold the penne and broccoli into the sauce (in the frypan if it fits, otherwise in a large bowl), stir in the parmesan, and rest 5 minutes so the pasta absorbs the sauce.
  6. Divide into 4 equal portions. Eat one now; cool the rest uncovered for 20 to 30 minutes, then cover and refrigerate.

Notes

  • Frank's RedHot or extra red pepper flakes can be added at the table or at work for heat without changing the whole batch.
