Method

  1. Whisk the eggs hard with the salt, black pepper, garlic powder and paprika until fully combined and slightly frothy.
  2. Heat the butter in a nonstick skillet over medium-low heat until it foams or shimmers.
  3. Pour in the eggs, let them sit a few seconds, then gently push from the edges to the center with a spatula to form soft curds.
  4. Pull off the heat while the eggs still look slightly wet; they finish cooking from residual heat. Serve right away.

Notes

  • Low heat is the key to creamy eggs.
  • Swap the seasonings freely: chili flakes, cumin, onion powder, Italian herbs, or everything bagel seasoning all work.
